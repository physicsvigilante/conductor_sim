## Purpose, physical limits and source attribution

M39a allows a teacher to **compose and revise** one bounded connected ideal metal with **1–8 independently named fully sealed mutually disjoint cavities** containing 0–16 named charges (each assigned to one cavity), homogeneous positive permittivity, zero external sources/fields and either isolated `FixedNetCharge` or zero-potential `Grounded`. Membership is a **teacher-declared topology fact**, not verified spatial point placement. No points, contours, boundaries, cavity radii, 3D field lines, local E(x), V(x), sigma(x), force, energy or time/current are qualified by this model.

For each cavity i with enclosed source total q_i, the **entire complete inner metal face total** is `-q_i`, irrespective of local shape/distribution. For a qualified isolated metal, `Q_outer=Q_metal+sum(q_i)`, `Phi_outer=Q_outer/epsilon`. For fully grounded metal with no exterior sources, `Q_outer=0`, `Q_metal=-sum(q_i)` and exterior E=0 by uniqueness. A whole-face zero never implies every point on that surface has zero sigma.
