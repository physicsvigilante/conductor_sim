# Composability contract — prospective architectural requirement

**Adopted for M22a and subsequent work.** Composability is an explicit,
continuously evaluated requirement, alongside the existing
[teaching-accompaniment evaluation contract](evaluation_contract.md).
This is prospective: historical contracts, signed/frozen evidence, schemas,
acceptance definitions and results retain their original meanings.

## Objective and scope

An unfamiliar arrangement of already-supported physical components should
ordinarily require **model authoring, not new problem-specific production
code**, provided its connections, global constraints, mathematical problem
and numerical regime are qualified. Native component availability alone does
not establish composability. A correct refusal is preferable to an invented
field, silently relaxed constraint or inappropriate solver fallback.

Keep separate: reusable physical components/laws; assembly and compatibility
rules; global geometry and constraints; mathematical investigations; numerical
qualification; observations; hypothetical revisions; and presentation.
Do not turn a requested plot, a potential-reference change, a numerical
initial guess or a teaching annotation into a physical condition.

Composability is assessed separately for:
- Fresh model assembly and structural revision of a retained model.
- Physical/global qualification and a well-posed mathematical investigation.
- Numerical solving, completion and independently checked physical behavior.
- Requested observations, comparisons and trustworthy presentation.

Success at one layer is not proof of success at the others. A valid model
can terminate at a guard; complete runs can have incompatible comparison
clocks/planes. Such outcomes are recorded separately.

## Reuse assessment before adding a problem family

Identify the existing laws, assembly operations, constraints, backend regimes
and observers. Explain precisely what is missing. Distinguish:
1. Existing-law combinations obstructed by an authoring/assembly gap.
2. New physical relationships or global boundary/geometry requirements.
3. Missing mathematical investigations or numerical qualification.
4. Observation/presentation limitations.
5. Repeated profile/version/dispatcher handling suitable for prospective reuse.

Implement a demonstrated reusable gap at its shared layer, not inside a
problem-specific path. Do not assume every restriction is unnecessary.
Conductor response, fixed-source superposition and effective mechanical
energy conventions must not be combined by naive field addition.

Choose the smallest bounded change that unlocks several unfamiliar qualified
arrangements. Do not introduce a universal graph compiler, generic callback
registry, solver merger or large refactor without demonstrated need.
Consolidate duplicated logic only when a common contract is established.
Legacy validation/serialization adapters may intentionally remain distinct.

## Continuous acceptance

Preselect positive, negative and structurally unfamiliar authoring cards
before changing production code. Fix the targeted relationships, qualification
boundaries and independent checks. Record any amendment rather than silently
dropping a failing card. A curated developer trial is not a blinded experiment
or a statistical estimate of JEE Advanced coverage.

For each card record: source or teacher-hypothetical provenance; public model
authoring path; structural lineage; physical qualification; numerical
completion; independent reference observations; requested observation status;
and failure/refusal stage and reason. Report distinct denominators. Correct
refusals must not inflate the successful-simulation count.

The unfamiliar cards must require no additional per-card production change
after the shared capability is written. Regression tests retain the input
models and exact legacy behavior where a historical contract requires it.
Run relevant acceptance in CI, not just a one-time screenshot demonstration.

Preserve original models and stable physical identities. Parameter revision,
component insertion/removal/replacement and analysis changes are distinct,
explicit operations. Requalify complete revised systems atomically. An
admissible baseline does not authorize every child arrangement. Replacements
must not silently redefine an existing physical identity.

## Teaching purpose and evidence

Stay close to JEE Advanced-relevant problem families and meaningful teaching
questions. Faithful interpretation, verified solving, honest visualization
and reuse for exploration are primary. Textbook-answer completeness is
separately evaluated under the existing contract, not a substitute for
physical correctness. Source-given facts and teacher hypotheticals remain
distinct. Static equilibrium changes are not elapsed-time dynamics; display
interpolation is not a physical simulation.

No historical evidence is rewritten, resigned, rescored or replaced by new
claims. New capabilities carry fresh evidence and explicit limitations.
