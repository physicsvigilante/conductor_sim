# M9 — Multiple external point charges around a conducting solid sphere

**Candidate.** The grounded and isolated M7/M8 one-source models are accepted
and remain supported. This milestone broadens **the same authored**
`ElectrostaticProblem(charges=(...), conductors=(SolidSphere(...),))` to
multiple external charge sites and independent comparison tests.

## Composition and capability boundaries

- The **analytical** `ExternalMultiSphereBackend` handles at least two authored
  charge components outside one grounded or fixed-net-charge `SolidSphere`.
  All sources must lie strictly outside the sphere; it accepts non-collinear
  positions anywhere in 3D. Exactly coincident sources are grouped by net
  charge, and cancelled sites are omitted. At least one active site must remain.
  Directly inspecting the analytical solution uses `sites` (grouped positions).
  `ExternalSphereBackend` automatically chooses the M7/M8 exact single-source
  reference or M9 multi-source reference; it does not invoke FEMM.
- The **numerical** `Femm5ExternalSphereBackend` uses the *same* physical model
  but admits at most 16 distinct nonzero-net sites on **one common axis
  through the sphere centre**. A deterministic axis is chosen automatically,
  even with sources on both sides of the sphere or rotated world coordinates.
  Non-collinear sites are rejected **before** any FEMM session is opened.
- Each numerical source is a separate small, independently fixed-Q conducting
  **sphere**, not an exact mathematical point and not a FEMM ring. Its radius
  is bounded by source-wall clearance, nearest-source separation and the
  configured regularization setting. FEMM measures the net source charge for
  each core. The physical sphere is separately grounded or assigned total Q.
  `solution.numerical_sources` describes all source cores; legacy
  `solution.source_radius_m` is defined only when there is a single active core.
- The existing viewer samples the same backend-independent `FieldSolution`
  interface; it masks every numerical source core, uses FEMM's one-sided field
  for numerical sigma, and exports precomputed states to offline HTML + JSON.
  There is no scene graph and **no browser-time charge motion/solve**.

## Independent electrostatic reference

Let the sphere radius be `a`, its centre `c`, its prescribed net charge `Q`
(if isolated), and external source `i` have strength `q_i`, world position
`r_i`, and distance `d_i = |r_i-c| > a`. Each source has an independent
Kelvin image:

- `q'_i = -q_i a/d_i`, located at `c + (a/d_i)^2 (r_i-c)`;
- grounded metal: central compensating image `q0 = 0`, total metal charge
  `Qmetal = sum(q'_i)`, metal potential `0`;
- isolated metal: an **additional central image** with
  `q0 = Q - sum(q'_i)`, actual net metal charge `Q`, and equipotential
  `Vmetal = q0/(4*pi*epsilon*a)`.

Outside the solid sphere, add the Coulomb potentials/fields of all real
sources, all Kelvin images and the optional central image. Inside the metal,
V is constant and E=0. Each surface-density contribution is
`-q_i*(d_i^2-a^2)/(4*pi*a*|a*n-(r_i-c)|^3)` for outward unit normal `n`,
plus uniform `q0/(4*pi*a^2)`. Integrating sigma over the whole sphere gives
exactly the conductor's total net charge. For non-collinear arrangements,
`surface_charge_density_at(face,world_surface_point)` is meaningful whereas
a **single-angle** polar profile is not.

The exact `conductor_force_on_charge_n(i)` excludes other real charges; the
exact `force_on_charge_n(i)` also includes direct real charge–charge forces
but excludes self-field. Indices refer to *grouped active sites*. The
`interaction_energy_j` includes real pair energy, the symmetric grounded
image term with factor 1/2, and for isolated spheres the extra quadratic
charging term with the sphere's constant Q self-energy excluded. Numerical
finite-difference tests verify `F_i = -grad_{r_i} U`. **FEMM does not independently
calculate force/energy in this milestone.**

## Required acceptance

1. Existing M1–M8 pure Python checks remain green. New exact tests verify
   image superposition, sphere equipotential, grounded and isolated Q,
   positive/negative charges, full spherical sigma integral even for 3D
   non-collinear positions, `E=-grad(V)`, `F=-grad(U)`, world-axis covariance,
   same-site grouping and strict unsupported cases.
2. A pinned real FEMM 5 build runs all **59 prior numerical regressions** plus
   **9 M9 cases**: grounded and several isolated Q configurations, individual
   measured external core charges, potential/field comparisons, induced density
   profile and flux, zero-net external dipole that must preserve both sites,
   axis/world rotation, mesh/domain/core-size sensitivity and the pre-session
   non-collinear refusal.
3. Chromium verifies the exported **four-state** analytical viewer and a real
   FEMM HTML/JSON export with no external requests or JavaScript errors.
   Correct labels and source data provenance are mandatory.

Numerical benchmark tolerances are representative, **not universal error
bounds**. FEMM finite source cores polarize and the finite Robin boundary
only approximates open space. The nearer-to-contact regime remains unvalidated.

**Still excluded:** non-collinear multi-source *numerical* FEMM problems,
multiple independent physical solid conductors, non-spherical/ground-plane
geometries, multiple charged bodies in contact, directly FEMM-measured
interaction forces, particle dynamics, adaptive error-controlled streamlines
and production-safe execution of untrusted authored Python.
