# M13a — Unified analytical forces and interaction energy

M13a adds one optional observation API without changing the authored
`ElectrostaticProblem` or making force support mandatory for `FieldSolution`.
It does not add a mechanics engine, trajectories, force arrows, or energy plots.

## Public API

```python
from conductor_sim import force_energy

solution = backend.solve(problem)  # the usual authored model and chosen backend
observed = force_energy(solution)

for index, charge in enumerate(observed.charges):
    print(index, charge.source.position_m, charge.source.charge_c)
    print(charge.direct_force_n)       # other real charges only
    print(charge.induced_force_n)      # polarization, including own images
    print(charge.background_force_n)   # fixed bias / original sphere charge
    print(charge.conductor_force_n)    # induced + background
    print(charge.total_force_n)        # direct + induced + background

print(observed.energy.direct_j)
print(observed.energy.induced_j)
print(observed.energy.background_j)
print(observed.energy.total_j)
print(observed.energy.reference)
print(observed.method, observed.image_order)
```

The immutable `ChargeForce`, `EnergyBreakdown`, and `ForceEnergyResult` records
contain SI values. `ForceEnergyResult.force_on_charge_n(index)` gives total
force and rejects negative/non-integer/boolean/out-of-range indices.
Indices identify **sorted grouped active sites**, not authored components or
persistent particle IDs. Exactly coincident charges are combined; zero-net
sites are omitted. Treating coincident components as separately moving charges
would introduce divergent mutual energies and is not licensed by this API.

`force_energy(solution)` adapts the existing analytical grounded-plane and
single/multiple exterior-sphere implementations, preserving their legacy
properties/methods. The new `ParallelPlateSolution.force_energy()` implements
the optional `ForceEnergyProvider` protocol. Custom solution classes can supply
the same method and result type without editing the observation dispatcher.

Parallel plates also expose `force_on_charge_n(index, include_direct=True)`
and the `interaction_energy_j` property. With `include_direct=False`, the
returned conductor force includes both induction and prescribed voltage bias.
Use the observation record for the **pure induced** contribution.

## Supported scope and explicit refusals

Supported analytical solutions: one grounded plane with its supported sources;
one grounded or fixed-net-charge solid sphere with external point charges;
and two parallel grounded/fixed-potential planes with charges inside the slab.
The last family includes charge-free/canceled cases (empty force tuple and
zero *interaction* energy), signed multi-source and fully 3D placements.
Existing backend geometry and active-source limits remain unchanged.

**Not a numerical FEMM force implementation.** Calling `force_energy` on a
FEMM solution raises `UnsupportedProblem` without sampling, closing, or
replacing it with an analytical result. Cavity/nested-shell solutions likewise
remain explicitly unsupported for this optional capability in M13a. Their
existing field/potential support is unaffected. Exceptions from custom
providers propagate rather than becoming zeros or fallback results.

## Physical definition and energy accounting

Write the regular field acting on source i as the sum of fields from other
real sources, induced conductor charge, and the conductor background present
when the external point charges are absent. With geometry and conductor
constraints fixed, the corresponding mechanical interaction potential is

    U = k * sum(i<j, q_i*q_j / |r_i-r_j|)
        + 1/2 * sum(i, q_i * phi_ind(r_i))
        + sum(i, q_i * phi_background(r_i))
    F_i = -gradient_{r_i}(U)

Here `k=1/(4*pi*epsilon)`. The singular *direct* self-field/energy is removed,
but the source's **own induced images remain**. The factor 1/2 applies only
to the induced contribution. Multiplying the background term by 1/2 would
incorrectly halve the force in a prescribed electric field.

For fixed-voltage electrodes this is the mechanical potential with the work
of maintaining the voltages accounted for, not simply the total field energy.
Point self energies and empty-conductor/capacitor constants are omitted.
In particular, a finite interaction energy between biased infinite plates
must not be labeled the total energy of their infinite uniform field.

For a fixed-Q isolated exterior sphere, the background is the original
monopole Q; the neutralizing central image induced by the external charges
belongs to polarization. The constant sphere self energy Q^2/(2C) is omitted.
The old single-source `induced_force_on_charge_n` property includes Q for
compatibility; the new `induced_force_n` separates it correctly.

With identical charge strengths, conductor geometry, constraints and voltage
gauge, the quasistatic external work of a displacement is `U_final-U_initial`;
the electric force does the opposite work. A common voltage shift adds
`sum(q_i)*shift` to U but changes neither the force nor such displacement work.
Do not subtract these energies across changing voltages, changing conductor
geometry, or different charge inventories and call that mechanical work.

## Parallel-plate kernel and finite-order symmetry

The infinite image Green function consists of positive images at h+2nd and
negative images at -h+2nd, where d is the gap and 0<h<d. A finite cutoff in the
old lower-plate-oriented sequence is reciprocal, but has a small residual
force at the true midpoint. M13a uses the **average of the two plate-oriented
cutoffs**, not a special-case zero at the midpoint.

Let `R(t)=sqrt(rho^2+t^2)`, source height h, observation height z. The kernel is

    G_N = sum(n=-N..N) [
          1/R(z-h-2nd)
        - 1/(2*R(z+h-2nd))
        - 1/(2*R(z+h-2(n+1)d)) ]

This is the average of the earlier cutoff and its reflection about the middle
of the slab. It is symmetric under source/observation exchange and under
swapping the two plates. Both properties hold at each finite N, so the
regular force is the derivative of the same energy and the symmetric
midpoint is unbiased (up to floating-point roundoff).

Potential, field, and new force/energy observations share this kernel. In
observations the real n=0 contribution is omitted from the induced kernel
and counted separately for i != j. Potentials use rationalized reciprocal
differences to limit distant-image cancellation. Exact conductor voltages,
source-induced integrated plate totals and zero metal fields remain as before.

**Finite N is still an approximation.** Interior near-boundary errors and
far-lateral errors are not uniformly bounded by this change; exact boundary
voltages are imposed separately. `image_order` remains 1..512, default 48.
Increase N and compare observations for quantitative work. Force/energy
consistency at one N does not by itself prove convergence to the infinite
solution. Existing M12 sample values can change slightly due to the symmetric
cutoff; the old unsymmetrized formula remains the same in the infinite limit.

## Independent checks and runnable example

```bash
python examples/force_energy.py --image-order 128 --output output/force_energy.json
python -m pytest tests/test_m13_observables.py -q
```

The example uses the **same observation call** for a grounded plane, grounded
sphere, isolated charged sphere, three positions in a grounded slab, and a
biased slab. It also calculates work for a fixed-voltage displacement. It
exports numerical observations, not a browser-time solver or animated motion.

The M13a regression suite checks:

- Plane `F=-k*q^2/(4*h^2)` and `U=-k*q^2/(4*h)`; sphere legacy agreement and
  correct separation of fixed Q from polarization.
- Independent slab force sums and the exact midpoint energy
  `U=-k*q^2*log(2)/d`; N-versus-2N convergence; near-plate single-plane limit;
  zero midpoint force and unstable normal equilibrium for either charge sign.
- Full 3D signed multi-source `F=-grad(U)`, including separate gradients for
  direct/induced/background terms; finite-difference regular-field limits;
  independent quadrature of work; reciprocity and plate-order invariance.
- World-coordinate transformations, charge/permittivity scaling, gauge shifts,
  cancellation/grouping, finite JSON, strict index validation, custom-provider
  propagation, and no analytical fallback or premature FEMM session cleanup.

Acceptance requires Python 3.10/3.12, retained browser/source checks and the
pinned live FEMM regression workflow. Its additional numerical test proves
honest refusal and session ownership, **not numerical force accuracy**. Exact
run IDs, source pins and counts are recorded in the associated PR acceptance
comment rather than inferred from mock tests.
