## What is physically qualified

An ideal fixed uniformly charged ring has two individually
reflection-invariant planar families:

1. **Meridian plane:** passes through the ring centre and contains its
   symmetry axis. The actual ring may intersect the moving charge's
   plane at two points; those are ideal wire singularities and need
   guarded exclusion.
2. **Equatorial plane:** the ring's own plane through its centre,
   with the physical plane normal parallel or antiparallel to the
   ring's axis. The ideal wire circle lies in that plane and still
   requires conservative guards.

`certify_ring_motion_plane` checks one actual typed ring using strict
world-coordinate and radius-based representation tolerances. Every
source in a composed inventory must separately preserve the authored
motion plane, or meet an already existing whole-inventory certificate
for paired point/rod charges. One or several full rings with different
axes can be combined when their **common** chosen plane is actually
invariant for every ring. Off-plane paired rings are not newly
admitted through an unproved collective symmetry inference.

The original `UniformRingAxisField` and `UniformDiskAxisField` remain
axis-only. Mixing them with a new `UniformRingField` in a motion
specification is refused until the complete shared axis-only field
contract can be proved. Pure legacy models stay unchanged.

The full 3D `F=qE` (and, when authored, `+mg`), with
`U=qV+Ug`, is evaluated at every actual RK stage. Existing numerical
normal-force checks prevent silent projection of a transverse
resultant. Changing an applied field later in an M24b schedule
requires **all** phases to preserve that same physical plane.
Changing ring source membership through M26a requires whole-model
reassembly and requalification; no source identity changes by
relabeling a drawing.

## Singular-wire and trajectory guards

The real source's `clearance_at` returns geometric 3D distance to
the ideal charged ring's wire, **not** a finite wire thickness or
physical collision. The existing RK launch/stage cutoff uses that
clearance and the authored source guard.

The new conservative RK **chord** test uses the 1-Lipschitz property
of the distance to any closed set: every point on a chord is at most
half its length from the chord midpoint. Thus a lower bound on its
minimum wire clearance is

`max(0, d(midpoint,ring) - chord_length/2)`.

If that bound reaches the exclusion distance, the shared controller
subdivides the tentative RK step. A near-wire physical run may
end with `source-guard`; neither an exact impact time nor a
bounce, absorption or charge transfer is invented. The chord/RK
stage guarantee does not pretend to certify every unsampled point
of the unknown exact differential equation solution.

## Source-faithful JEE teaching example

The actual source workbook `02_conductors_half_size.pdf`,
physical p12 linked Q40–41, specifies Ring A/B with:
signed ring charges +10 µC and −20 µC; radius 0.30 m each;
centres 0.40 m apart on world x; symmetry axes world x;
and external **upward** uniform E of 100,000 V/m (+world y).

The workbook Q41's mass is symbolic; a numerical free trajectory
cannot be claimed as that problem's exact path without additional
physical conditions. For the explicit **teacher hypothetical** in
`examples/m27b_offaxis_motion.py --case workbook-xy`, the moving
probe charge is +10 µC, and numeric mass **0.01 kg**, initial
position **(.2,0,0) m**, initial velocity **(.1,0,0) m/s**,
duration **.015 s**, source guard .001 m and physical
world x–y plane are explicitly teacher-supplied. With
`plane_normal=(0,0,-1)`, the viewer screen right is physical
+x and screen up is physical +y. The transverse applied force
therefore produces actual upward acceleration and curved
physical motion; the previous fake 1D path was correctly refused.

A different teacher variation `--case driven-switch` adds a
real stationary-medium linear drag and a +y 20 kV/m uniform
applied-field change at .008 s. The original source-given
100 kV/m fixed external field remains intact and the additional
switch is separately attributed. Saved left/right switch states
are **continuous position and velocity** but have new force,
without a fabricated impulse or electromagnetic propagation.

independently quadrature-checked off-axis field/force, a
test-only velocity-Verlet trajectory reference, both reflection
plane families, arbitrary world-frame rotation, original
workbook transverse field, gravity, scheduled driven force/work,

axis-only fields and unsupported old viewer exports.

The reserved test uses a *different*, noncoaxial two-ring model
with gravity and another independently written Coulomb-line
velocity-Verlet reference. Frozen inputs, tolerances and
first-execution outcomes are preserved distinctly from a rerun.
Full Python 3.10/3.12 tests, all real FEMM 5 *conductor
nonregressions*, and actual Chromium acceptance are mandatory
before independent R2 review. FEMM is not the solver or a new
numerical reference for ideal nonconducting ring motion.

No fully free 3D charged-particle dynamics, collective off-plane
ring-pair certification, moving source charges, conductor
induction, off-axis disc fields, contact/rebound, magnetic Lorentz
force, a general-purpose scene graph, automatic PDF problem
interpretation or statistical JEE-coverage claim is included.
