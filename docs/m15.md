# M15a and M15b — force and energy of charges inside conducting cavities

## Scope and composability

The existing analytical spherical-cavity solutions already supply exact
Kelvin-image potentials, fields and induced inner-surface charge density.
M15a extends the **optional** `force_energy(solution)` observation API (rather
than altering the field solvers) to these analytical solution families:

- `ConcentricShellSolution`: a grouped net point source at the cavity centre;
- `ImageChargeShellSolution`: one arbitrary off-centre source;
- `MultiImageShellSolution`: zero or multiple active 3D sources in one cavity;
- `NestedShellSolution`: zero or multiple sources in the innermost cavity of
  multiple concentric, separated conducting shells, with independent
  grounded/fixed-net-charge constraints.

`Grounded()` and `FixedNetCharge(q)` shell constraints are retained, with
original `ElectrostaticProblem` components and the standard `ChargeForce`,
`EnergyBreakdown`, `ForceEnergyResult` data objects. Sources at exactly the
same location are grouped; canceled sites do not become fictitious particles.
The older single-source/spherical-symmetry families adapt their existing
saved solution results to the same Kelvin observation formula, without
starting a second solve. **There is no new FEMM force/energy capability.**

M15b permits the same `PositionSweepSpec` and `sample_position_sweep` machinery
to move one explicitly selected *authored* point charge along a straight path
strictly inside the vacuum cavity. A spherical cavity is convex, so both
strictly interior endpoints ensure the full straight segment lies inside.
Paths that reach the metal face or intersect another authored point charge
are rejected. Nested-shell backends still reject unsupported nonconcentric
shapes, sources in inter-shell gaps, and other unsupported domains. The fixed
slice now works even for a charge initially **exactly at the cavity centre**.
No new renderer, scene graph, browser-time solver, or mechanical time integrator
was introduced. The precomputed HTML retains synchronized force arrows,
force-versus-position, mechanical-energy-versus-position, position controls,
quasistatic external work, conductor-density profiles, and existing field-line
and potential-colour controls.

## Independent physical model and reference

Let a spherical cavity have inner radius \(a\), centre \(c\), and a charge
\(q_j\) at \(r_j\), where \(d_j=|r_j-c|<a\). Its Kelvin image satisfies

\[
q'_j=-\frac{a}{d_j}q_j,
\qquad r'_j=c+\frac{a^2}{d_j^2}(r_j-c).
\]

At \(d_j=0\), this external image goes to infinity while retaining the exact
regular potential limit \(-kq_j/a\) and **zero** image field inside.
The direct Coulomb self term is excluded from force and mechanical energy,
but the charge's **own** Kelvin image is retained. For grouped active sites
\(i\), the local induced force follows

\[
F_{i,\mathrm{ind}}=kq_i\sum_j q'_j\,
\frac{r_i-r'_j}{|r_i-r'_j|^3}.
\]

The corresponding reciprocal image potential is used to compute the
half-weighted induced interaction energy. The inner metal potential
\(V_\mathrm{inner}\) is spatially constant inside the cavity; for a given
fixed source inventory its value does **not** change with the positions of
those sources. It may contain a piece from the shell net charges and outer
conductor conditions, and another constant from the sources' induced outer
charge. To keep components physically distinguished:

\[
U_{\rm direct}=k\sum_{i<j}\frac{q_iq_j}{|r_i-r_j|},
\]

\[
U_{\rm induced}=\tfrac12\sum_i q_i\phi_{\rm image}(r_i)
+\tfrac12 Q_{\rm sources}[V_{\rm inner}-V_{\rm background}],
\]

\[
U_{\rm background}=Q_{\rm sources}V_{\rm background}.
\]

Here \(V_{\rm background}\) is the uniform innermost shell potential
of the **same conductor constraints with all interior point charges removed**.
For a single isolated shell with net charge \(Q_{\rm shell}\), it is
\(k Q_{\rm shell}/b\), where \(b\) is the outer shell radius.
For a grounded shell it is zero. For nested shells it is obtained by the
existing exact radial-conductor solver with its same conductor constraints,
not by applying an arbitrary gauge shift. These constant energy terms have
zero force and cancel in quasistatic displacement work when charges,
conductor geometry, grounding/charge constraints and voltage gauge stay fixed.
They are **not** the total electrostatic energy of all conductors, and do not
represent kinetic energy, source dynamics, or an energy of a moving wire.

For **one** charge \(q\) at displacement \(r\) from the cavity centre, the
exact force is radial and independent of the surrounding shell net charge:

\[
\mathbf F_{\rm ind}=kq^2\frac{a\mathbf r}{(a^2-r^2)^2},
\qquad
U_{\rm image}=-\frac{kq^2a}{2(a^2-r^2)}.
\]

The cavity centre has **zero induced force** and is an **unstable equilibrium
under radial displacement**, regardless of the charge's sign. Force and
potential energy are independently checked against textbook point-image
positions/charges, signed multi-source superposition, and finite-difference
\(-\nabla_{r_i}U\) in three dimensions. Increased exterior shell radius,
net charge, or grounding can modify the position-independent interaction
energy baseline but cannot alter the cavity force when the inner geometry
and internal charge locations remain fixed.

## Reproduce with the existing generic explorer

```bash
python examples/force_energy_explorer.py --case cavity-grounded --steps 7 --output output/m15-grounded.html
python examples/force_energy_explorer.py --case cavity-isolated --steps 7 --output output/m15-isolated.html
python examples/force_energy_explorer.py --case cavity-nested --steps 7 --output output/m15-nested.html
python examples/force_energy_explorer.py --case cavity-multi --steps 7 --output output/m15-multi.html
python examples/force_energy_explorer.py --case cavity-centered --steps 7 --output output/m15-centered.html
```

The displayed movement is a **position scan through separate static equilibria**;
it is not a simulated particle trajectory. The centre case deliberately has
no force arrow at the first zero-force frame, and displays one as the charge
moves off-centre. The nested case uses the **same authoring interface** as
one grounded shell. The multiple-source example is authored in the display
plane so field-line tracing remains valid; general 3D off-slice fields may
correctly have no valid 2D field lines.

## Acceptance boundaries

M15a/b introduces analytical-only observations and reusable cavity paths. It
does **not** add force observations for numerical FEMM 5 solutions, moving
particles in time, arbitrary particle masses or velocities, curved 3D paths,
charges in metal, connected moving conductors or solids with external charges.
There is no invented numerical force and no fallback to the cavity analytical
solver when a FEMM solution is requested.

Required gates: independent image-charge/symmetry/energy-gradient and charge
conservation checks; all earlier Python 3.10/3.12 tests; browser acceptance of
several M15 cases (including the centre's zero-force arrow behavior), and the
existing pinned **real FEMM 5** regression suite with zero numerical skips.
The FEMM suite establishes **no regression** to existing numerical physics,
not numerical accuracy of the new analytical force capability. Previous
finite-series parallel-plate accuracy and excluded-source rendering limitations
remain separately documented.

**Historical M13c note:** its original open trial correctly marked cavity
force/energy unavailable *at the time*. Its frozen score is still a historical
engineering record and must not be advertised as a fresh present-day capability
classification after M15.
