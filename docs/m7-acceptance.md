## Independent grounded-sphere predictions versus FEMM

Physical grounded solid sphere radius **0.12 m**, source charge **+1 nC** placed on the positive symmetry axis at the indicated distance from sphere centre, uniform vacuum. The FEMM numerical source is a finite, separately charged small conductor; the image charge is **not** supplied to the solver.

| Source distance d | Exact sphere induced net charge | FEMM measured induced net charge | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.21 m | −0.571428571 nC | −0.575973201 nC | 0.80% |
| 0.27 m | −0.444444444 nC | −0.447367826 nC | 0.66% |
| 0.34 m | −0.352941176 nC | −0.355091918 nC | 0.61% |

The exact image-charge relation is `Q_ind = -q*a/d`. FEMM independently reports **0 V** physical grounded sphere potential in all three exported states. Inner surface charge is *zero by geometry* for a solid conductor (no hollow cavity); `ConductorResult` uses `inner=0`, `outer=measured Q_ind`.

The exact attractive force along the source-facing axis is **−2.56761×10⁻⁷ N**, **−8.50892×10⁻⁸ N**, and **−3.58047×10⁻⁸ N** at the respective distances. These force values come from the **analytical image-charge formula**, **not** a FEMM force integration. Unit tests independently verify `F=-dU/dd` and `E=-∇V`.

## What the live tests verify

- Real FEMM source and physical conductor charge, grounded potential and conductor interior field, both positive and negative external source charges.
- Exterior potential/vector field against the independent Kelvin-image reference at multiple vacuum points and coordinate orientations.
- Stronger negative induced surface density on the near face than the far face, with local one-sided FEMM field estimates checked against exact angular density and a coarse independently sampled `σ` integral.
- Core radius, outer-domain Robin boundary and mesh sensitivity. The exterior has multipole content, so the finite Robin domain is only an asymptotic approximation.
- World-coordinate translation/rotation covariance and an actual FEMM-generated HTML+JSON export; browser checks for the self-contained output.
- All earlier M2–M6b FEMM tests remain green.

The live M7 numerical test limits allow up to **14%** relative error on physical induced net charge; **22%** relative plus **3 V** absolute on sampled potential; **38%** relative plus **100 V/m** absolute on vector E; **65%** relative plus `1e-10 C/m²` absolute on sampled local density; **40%** relative on sampled density integral; and **20%** potential sensitivity for tested FEMM mesh/domain/core variants. These are benchmark tolerances, not maximum errors throughout the domain.

## Historical benchmark crosswalk

The M6a PDF case `0p61-external-grounded-sphere`0 was correctly classified
as unsupported **at the M6a baseline**. M7 adds genuine support for that
physics using the new typed solid-sphere model and numerical backend, without
relocating the external charge into a hollow cavity. M6a and M6b case scores
remain frozen to document what was achievable at the time; they are **not**
live current capability reports. No other blocked M6a geometries are claimed
as newly supported here.

## Explicit limitations

**Accepted:** one nonzero external point-charge position, one 
`SolidSphere(radius, Grounded())`, a uniform dielectric, real FEMM axisymmetry. The mathematical image-charge reference itself is exact for arbitrary charge/sphere orientation and separation `d>a` within this physical domain.

**Not accepted:** isolated charged sphere near the charge, multiple external charges, eccentric or arbitrary conductors, charged planes, ring distributions, finite-core point-source limit at near-contact gaps, direct boundary-element surface-charge integration, FEMM force or time-domain charge trajectory simulation, production-grade solver supervision. Field and potential are not defined at the ideal source; numeric queries inside the FEMM source core are explicitly excluded. Existing interior-shell backends remain separately accepted and are not widened by M7.
