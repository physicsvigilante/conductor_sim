# M21a — JEE Advanced-first electrostatics coverage and next priorities

## Purpose and evidence

This audit responds to the request to **stay close to meaningful JEE Advanced
problems**. Its role is to choose the next *physical* work, not to build another
scenario-management layer.

The cohort was locked **before** source inspection in [the charter](m21a_charter.md):
18 exact page/question anchors in the 17-page scanned
`02_conductors_half_size.pdf`, plus 11 complementary examples in the 71-page
`POTENTIAL_CONDUCTORS1.pdf`. Both checked-in PDFs were verified against pinned
SHA-256 digests and original page counts, and all source pages in the evidence
packet were rendered as complete images. The searchable PDF's selected pages
were also extracted as text. The scanned PDF did not provide usable native
text; its diagrams were reviewed visually, without fabricated OCR.

**This is a selected 29-item engineering assessment, not 29 end-to-end
simulations, an automatic PDF parser, or a representative JEE frequency
survey.** Source figures, source-authored quantities and source-qualified
physics are differentiated from teacher hypotheticals. Workbook exercises,
worked teaching illustrations and printed past-paper archives are distinct.

Machine-readable case-by-case matrix: [m21a_coverage.json](m21a_coverage.json).
Reproducibility: `python tools/m21a_audit.py --out output/m21a-report.json`.
The report pins both original PDFs and validates the 29-item inventory.

### What has actually improved since the old PDF audits

- **Concentric wiring is no longer a blanket blocker.** M14a/M14b support
  floating/grounded links between concentric conducting shells, including a
  solid inner sphere plus shell. The original scanned p3 Q27 (`20 μC`,
  radii 10/20 cm) has an independently checked field-energy release near
  **9 J**. Linked p10 Q13–14 and p14 numerical Q7 are also now supported
  by existing before/after equilibrium APIs. This is **not** a
  time-resolved discharge simulation.
- **Exterior charge and conducting sphere** are supported via Kelvin images,
  with optional real FEMM 5 checks. Scanned p8 multiple Q21 and p9 multiple
  Q27 already have independent source-anchored M13c checks. For p8 Q21,
  a sequence of static snapshots supplies induced charge/force, but does
  **not** directly supply a current waveform.
- **Prescribed fixed sources and real particle motion** exist via M19–M20,
  including axial rings/discs, point-charge superposition, a uniformly charged
  volume sphere and a separate nonconducting shell. They are not metal
  conductor models, nor are off-axis ring/disc fields available.

### The source-fidelity distinctions that matter most

1. **Scanned p12 linked Q40–41: rings A/B.** The actual ring axes are
   horizontal world x, with an *upward* external E field. For the specific
   Q40 endpoint-work question, the existing axial ring potentials give
   `W = q(V_A - V_B) ≈ +3.595 J` (**3.6 J**); the uniform vertical
   field does zero work on the horizontal displacement. This is a
   source-faithful **potential/work calculation**. It is *not* legitimate
   to simulate a free particle confined to that axis: the transverse
   applied force is real, and no physical guide is specified. A work
   explorer should show endpoints/source identity without faking a
   trajectory. Do not silently assign a numerical mass to the symbolic
   Q41 situation.
2. **Searchable notes p13 Illustration 7: symmetric fixed charges.** Two
   equal held charges at `(0,+a)` and `(0,-a)` produce an invariant x
   trajectory for a launched opposite-sign particle by reflection symmetry.
   Point-source V/E and turning energy are already available. But the
   current motion-plane certifier requires *each* source to lie in the
   selected plane, so it refuses a physically legitimate symmetric
   superposition. A small **symmetry-certificate** capability is more
   appropriate than projecting away unmodelled forces.
3. **Scanned p2 Q15 and notes p68 Illustration 4: widely separated
   connected spheres.** Both explicitly assume large separation, so the
   JEE-scale leading model is equality of potential with
   `Q_i ∝ R_i` and conserved total charge, not the exact 3D induction
   of touching/nonconcentric conductors. M14's concentric capacitance
   matrix **must not** be applied to separated objects. A new, explicitly
   asymptotic connected-sphere model would be small and reusable.
4. **Scanned p11 Q28 and notes p70 Illustration 9: multiple irregular
   cavities.** Gauss-law total induced charges can be reasoned about now,
   but correct local surface charge distributions/fields need genuine
   nonconcentric 3D boundary physics. A symmetric shell rendering would
   misteach the geometry.
5. **Actual IIT-JEE 2012 p15 archive Q2:** the released proton between
   *vertical* parallel plates has both `qE` horizontally and `mg`
   vertically. Its 45° initial acceleration is fixed by `qE=mg`;
   the expected voltage is around `10⁻⁹ V`. Electric-only M19 motion
   is incomplete. Source provenance crosschecked against independent
   IIT-JEE 2012 paper text:
   https://questions.examside.com/past-years/jee/question/two-large-vertical-and-parallel-metal-plates-having-a-separa-2012-marks-4-kxpcyrhftjkbo5ij.htm
6. **Actual scanned JEE Advanced 2020 oil-drop archive:** the droplet
   first approaches terminal speed before the switch and applied fields
   leave it floating. Static `mg+qE=0` is a useful teaching slice;
   reproducing the entire story needs gravity, drag and a qualified
   switched-field protocol. No finite-disc fringe field, drag or actual
   time-domain current is claimed.

## Additional archived questions noticed outside the locked 29-case cohort

These are *not* silently added to the audit denominator. They identify
particularly direct acceptance targets for subsequent implementation.

- **IIT-JEE 2012, scanned p15 archive Q1**: graphs of E(r) and V(r) for a
  uniformly charged **thin surface shell**. The current
  `UniformShellField` formula is physically adequate; the pedagogical
  missing piece is a source-faithful radial graph with E's surface jump.
  An independent M21a test checks both sides and the undefined surface E.
- **JEE Advanced 2021 Paper 1, scanned p16 archive Q9–10**: two fixed
  charges `−Q` at (0,0) and `+Q/√3` at (2,0) yield the `V=0`
  equipotential `(x−3)²+y²=3` with radius `√3 m`, centre `(3,0)`.
  The existing fixed-point superposition correctly evaluates V on this
  locus (independently checked), but the prescribed-source viewer **does
  not paint a physically sampled 2D equipotential map**. Verified source:
  https://jeeadv.ac.in/past_qps/2021_1_English.pdf
- **IIT-JEE 2011, scanned p16 archive**: two distant conducting objects
  connected by a wire, supporting the need for an explicitly qualified
  far-separated capacitance relationship.
- **JEE Advanced 2019, scanned p16 archive**: small *hole* in an insulating
  charged shell—a partial-surface charge-distribution problem, not the
  uniform full shell; requires new physics to show the hole's local field.
- **IIT-JEE 2012, scanned p16 hexagon**: discrete fixed-point-charge
  superposition and potential symmetry; current physics is capable,
  but a source-faithful planar field/potential explorer is missing.

## Recommended next milestone (highest near-term yield)

**M21b — JEE source-faithful electric-potential and field explorer.**

Build **one small observer/viewer adapter** over the *existing* verified
`potential_at`/`electric_field_at` contracts:
- For full-spatial prescribed fixed sources (point charges, fixed rods,
  full spheres/shells, uniform E), sample a **certified physical 2D slice**
  and display real V equipotentials and E arrows. Keep singular/undefined
  regions masked. Never paint an off-axis ring/disc field.
- For radial/spherical or axis-only sources, show true qualified
  E-coordinate/V-coordinate profiles and surface jumps; no invented
  off-axis field.
- Support explicit **source-given endpoint work** `q(V_A−V_B)` without
  fabricating elapsed-time motion or mechanical track constraints.
- Anchor three acceptance problems: 2021 Q9/10 equipotential circle, 2012
  thin shell E/V radial graphs, workbook p12 Q40 ring endpoint work.
- Reuse the existing procedural renderer and authoring identities; no
  general scene graph, browser solver, numerical ODE changes or arbitrary
  3D field engine. Include analytic reference curves and genuine Chromium
  physics/legibility tests.

**Why first?** It supplies substantial teaching visualization for multiple
real archived questions with **already-qualified physics**, at lower
implementation risk than a new conductor geometry solver. It also creates
the field/potential visualization foundation for more complex charge-motion
problems. It should not delay a subsequent physics expansion indefinitely.
