# M18a — One moving charge and externally held fixed point charges

## Goal and authoring contract

Compose new JEE electrostatic-motion arrangements using **the same**
`ElectrostaticProblem`, `PointCharge`, conductor geometry, analytical backend,
`force_energy`, `Motion2DSpec` and `integrate_motion_2d` that already power
M13–M17. Do **not** introduce a new electrostatic law or integration algorithm.

Exactly **one chosen authored charge** moves. All others are *externally held
fixed* at their authored coordinates. Their reaction forces are balanced by
ideal supports; they do not receive acceleration or move. Their zero
displacement means the supports do no mechanical work. The existing
`EnergyBreakdown` includes moving–held, held–held and all conductor-mediated
contributions; any constant held–held interaction/self-image energies cancel
when calculating the moving particle's mechanical energy drift.

```python
from conductor_sim import (
    ConductingPlane, ElectrostaticProblem, Grounded, GroundedPlaneBackend,
    Motion2DSpec, PointCharge, integrate_motion_2d,
)

problem = ElectrostaticProblem(
    charges=(
        PointCharge(-0.5e-9, (0.05, 0.0, 0.12)),  # held source, index 0
        PointCharge(+1.0e-9, (0.00, 0.0, 0.10)),  # moving source, index 1
    ),
    conductors=(ConductingPlane(constraint=Grounded()),),
)
spec = Motion2DSpec(problem, charge_index=1, mass_kg=1e-6,
                    initial_velocity_m_s=(0.12, 0.0, -0.01),
                    duration_s=0.03, sample_interval_s=0.005)
trajectory = integrate_motion_2d(spec, GroundedPlaneBackend())
print(trajectory.samples[-1].position_m, trajectory.termination)
```

You may author the moving charge at **any original charge index**; the backend's
lexicographic grouping order does not redefine it. Each internal stage creates
an immutable `problem_at(position_m)` with only that selected component
replaced. The full unchanged charge inventory goes to the **existing**
analytical backend. A `ForceEnergyResult` is accepted only if it reports
exactly those original active sites, with their charges and locations.
The net force on the selected source drives the **existing adaptive RK4**.
No analytic reference is substituted for unavailable FEMM force observations.

## Proven planar domain

To retain scientifically meaningful two-dimensional dynamics (not impose a
fictitious guide), conductors and **all held sites** must share a physically
invariant reflection-symmetry plane. The plane includes the chosen source's
initial position and its initial velocity. Supported conductor arrangements
are unchanged:

- One grounded infinite plane, or two facing parallel infinite planes with
  grounded/fixed-potential constraints (the existing finite-order reciprocal
  image series remains approximate where applicable).
- One grounded or prescribed-net-charge solid conducting sphere, with all
  point charges in the exterior vacuum.
- One or more disjoint concentric spherical shells with all charges strictly
  in the innermost cavity, with the existing grounded/fixed-Q constraints.

A degenerate normal/radial launch may use the held charge locations to choose
an azimuth. Mixed, off-plane or intersecting geometries are explicitly
refused, rather than silently projecting a 3D force into a chosen slice.
There may be up to **32 distinct nonzero active sites** (one moving plus at
most 31 held). Coincident authored sites must first be combined by the author
into a single physical `PointCharge`; this motion API will not silently group
or cancel components and lose identity.

## Vacuum, source approach and energy guards

`surface_guard_m` retains the M17 conductor approach cutoff. New
`charge_guard_m` is an optional positive pairwise separation cutoff that
applies **only** between the moving particle and held ideal point charges.
Its default is `max(1e-8 * geometry_scale, 1e-5 * smallest_initial_separation)`.
The guarded point-to-point distance, conservative timestep bound, rejected
RK stages and local line-segment checks prevent stepping through the
singular point-source region. If a guard is approached, the result terminates
as `charge-guard` or `surface-guard`, records a matching event and explicitly
says **not an exact collision**. No bounce, fusion or finite-size particle
impact physics is invented.

`Motion2DResult.as_dict()` keeps the existing
`conductor_sim.motion_2d.v1` time-series schema and records additional
**M18a-only** fields when fixed sources exist: `moving_charge_index`,
`stationary_charges` (each with original `charge_index`, signed `charge_c`
and unchanged `position_m`), `charge_guard_m` and the fixed-support
assumption. The original single-charge serialization remains unchanged.
The shared saved-result semantic validator recognizes this extended
physics-only payload, including the distinct guard event and pairwise
separation, but does not cryptographically authenticate or re-integrate an
arbitrarily edited history.

A fixed-voltage conductor's `interaction_energy_j` is the original
**effective mechanical interaction potential**, including voltage-source
work—not the raw (infinite, for infinite plates) capacitor field energy.
Numerical drift uses separate direct, induced and background energy
differences, as in M17. The model remains Newtonian below 0.1c and
assumes quasistatic electrostatics. Radiative effects, actual moving-support
work, magnetic forces and velocities for the held charges are not modeled.

## No premature multi-charge viewer

M18a produces genuine **physics/JSON only**. `sample_motion_2d()` and
`motion_slice_2d()` explicitly refuse M18a multi-charge specifications,
*before* solving or preparing a misleading HTML export. M17b's one-charge
viewer remains unchanged. M18b must render both held and moving sources
and validate every displayed field frame against the original full inventory.

## Independent validation and acceptance

Regression tests cover full authored-index tracking (including index 1 and
permutation), an independent two-source grounded-plane mirror-charge force,
independent exterior-sphere Kelvin images (grounded/fixed-Q), mechanical
potential finite differences, fixed-voltage plates, radial launch with a held
off-axis charge, a centered-cavity moving charge in a fixed-source field,
concentric nested cavities, source-approach termination, malformed/duplicate
inventory, no FEMM fallback and solution cleanup. All existing M16/M17
one-charge tests, numerical semantics and browser checks remain retained.

Merge requires exact-head Python 3.10 and 3.12, full retained
visualization/Chromium and M15c/M17c authoring workflows, and the pinned
**97 real-FEMM numerical regressions with zero skips**. This numerical gate
establishes *non-regression*, not new FEMM force/trajectory capability.
