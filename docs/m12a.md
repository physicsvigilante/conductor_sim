# M12a — Parallel infinite conducting plates: analytical reference

## Reuse and authoring

M12a composes **two existing** `ConductingPlane` half-space components within
`ElectrostaticProblem`; their inward normals must be strictly opposing and
their open positive-normal regions must overlap in a finite slab. Source
`PointCharge` components may be placed at arbitrary 3D positions **strictly
between** the plates, with up to 32 distinct nonzero grouped sites.
`FixedPotential(volts)` is added for plane electrodes, alongside
`Grounded()`. `FixedNetCharge` is still invalid for infinite planes, and
`FixedPotential` is deliberately **not** supported for spherical conductors.
The one-grounded-plane analytical and FEMM backends retain their old domains;
a single `FixedPotential` plane is not silently treated as `Grounded`.

For normals `n` pointing into the vacuum from plate A, gap `d`, and
plate voltages `Va,Vb`, the charge-free solution is **exact**:

    V_bg(z) = Va + (Vb - Va) * z/d
    E_bg = -n * (Vb - Va)/d

Here `z = dot(x - plane_A.point_m, n)`. Either plate can be authored first,
provided both normals face the slab. The entire field is 3D-covariant under
rigid translations and rotations.

## Image-charge reference with point sources

For a charge `q` at transverse coordinate `rho0`, axial position
`0 < h < d`, and a field point in the gap, the grounded-slab contribution
is evaluated as the symmetric truncated series

    V_q(rho,z) = q/(4*pi*eps) * sum(n=-N..N) [
        1 / sqrt(|rho-rho0|^2 + (z-h-2*n*d)^2)
      - 1 / sqrt(|rho-rho0|^2 + (z+h-2*n*d)^2)
    ]

Add `V_bg` and all source contributions; `E=-grad(V)` is evaluated term
by term. `image_order=N` defaults to 48 and can be configured from 1 to
512. This finite series is an **approximation**, not an exact field or an
a-priori uniform error bound. Error depends on N, source placement, and sample
location; increase N and compare results when accuracy matters. The potential
on either ideal conductor is explicitly fixed to its Dirichlet voltage; field
sampling on the metal boundary raises `SingularField` because its limits
differ. The ideal source position itself is singular.

Surface density on the vacuum face is `sigma = eps * (E_vacuum dot n_face)`,
where `n_face` points into the slab and `E_vacuum` is the series evaluated
one-sided at the surface. Specify `conductor_name` because there are two
planes with distinct surfaces.

## Infinite area and reported charge totals

If `Va = Vb`, there is no uniform capacitor field; the exact *finite*
source-induced integrated charge on the two infinitely extended faces is

    Q_A = -sum_i(q_i * (1-h_i/d))
    Q_B = -sum_i(q_i * h_i/d)

These follow from the exact weighting potentials and **do not** depend on
the truncated image-series order. Their sum is minus the real charge total.
If `Va != Vb`, the uniform capacitor contribution has nonzero charge
density over an **infinite area**. Total electrode charge is unbounded:
`ConductorResult.net_charge_c` and `outer_surface_charge_c` are `None`
(serialized `null`, rendered as `n/a`), not 0, infinity or NaN.
The local finite `sigma(rho)` remains available. These are ideal infinite
half-spaces, **not** finite-area plate charge or capacitance calculations.

## Viewer and capability boundaries

The existing plane geometry, metal masks, surface-density profiles, numerical
field-sampling pipeline, and self-contained HTML/JSON playback are reused:

```bash
python -m pytest -m 'not femm5' -q
python examples/parallel_plates.py --output output/parallel_plates.html
```

The example shows a charge-free 20 V capacitor, a positive charge between
grounded electrodes, and two signed charges between grounded electrodes.
All three are **stored static equilibria**; slider playback is not moving
charge dynamics, and no scene graph or browser-time solver is introduced.
Analytical charge-free field lines are drawn using authored visualization
seeds rather than imaginary charges.

**Not M12a:** no claim of exact finite-order image fields, no FEMM solution
between two plates, no non-axisymmetric numerical geometry, no force/energy
API for this slab yet, no finite plates/fringing, no dielectrics or connected
electrodes, and no automatic text-to-model parser. M12b will add a finite
FEMM 5 geometry, axisymmetric charged source cores, independently measured
electrode/source properties and explicit finite-radius boundary error tests.

## Acceptance policy

M12a acceptance requires ordinary Python 3.10/3.12 checks, source/browser
validation and a sample export on the exact candidate branch. FEMM tests
belong to the retained M1–M11 regression workflow; **they do not establish
a parallel-plate FEMM capability**. Do not merge a candidate with red checks.
