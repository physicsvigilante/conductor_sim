# M7 — Point charge outside a grounded solid conducting sphere

**Status: live-validated within the bounded M7 domain.** See [M7 acceptance and observed errors](m7-acceptance.md).

## Why a new component?

`SphericalShell` means a conductor with an actual empty interior cavity. A
JEE grounded solid sphere is instead authored as `SolidSphere(radius_m,
Grounded(),...)`. The two objects are not confused, even though an empty,
grounded spherical-shell exterior obeys an equivalent external boundary-value
problem. Existing M1–M6b geometries remain unchanged.

## Exact reference, independent of FEMM

For conductor radius `a`, an external ideal point charge `q` displaced by
`d>a` from the sphere center (unit direction `n`), the grounded sphere
exterior is represented by a Kelvin image inside the metal:

- Image charge `q' = -q a/d` at `x' = center + (a²/d) n`.
- Exterior potential `V(x) = (q/|x-x_q| + q'/|x-x'|)/(4π ε)`.
- Inside metal `V=0`, `E=0`; field is undefined on the conducting surface.
- Induced sphere net charge is `Q_sphere=q'` (not `-q`).
- Surface density at polar angle θ from the charge-facing axis:
  `σ(θ) = -q(d²-a²)/(4π a (d²+a²-2ad cosθ)^(3/2))`.
- Exact force on external charge from induced sphere charge:
  `F = -n q² a d/[4π ε (d²-a²)²]` (always attractive, regardless of sign).
- Electrostatic interaction energy is `U = -(q² a)/(8π ε (d²-a²))`;
  the factor `1/2` reflects induced-charge readjustment and yields `F=-dU/dd`.

`ExternalGroundedSphereBackend` computes these reference quantities. The
surface charge integral matches `-q a/d`. Direct and image singularities
are not sampled. M7 itself covers one nonzero point charge strictly outside a grounded
`SolidSphere`; [M8](m8.md) subsequently extends the shared external solver to
isolated spheres. Multiple external charges are still unsupported.

## Numerical FEMM 5 vertical slice

`Femm5ExternalSphereBackend` uses existing `Femm5Session`,
`Femm5Settings`, FEMM 5's native electrostatics geometry/compiler commands and
postprocessing. The real model is one grounded physical sphere plus one
separate tiny fixed-charge **numerical sphere** at the external source.
The numerical source is not a 2D off-axis point (which would be a ring when
revolved), nor is its finite radius an exact mathematical point. It is excluded
from queries and must be checked under mesh/core-size variation. The physical
sphere is assigned a fixed-potential conductor boundary and its interior is
meshed as source-free dielectric held at zero potential by the boundary.

The independent source axis is automatically selected from sphere center
to charge, so 3D world rotation/translation requires no authored FEMM
commands. For the outer computational boundary, a mixed/Robin condition
approximates decay to zero infinity. Since external source plus induced
charge has higher multipoles, this **finite** domain condition is approximate:
outer-boundary sensitivity is an explicit acceptance test.

Real FEMM directly measures the physical sphere net charge/potential and
numerical source net charge; its local sphere density is estimated from
**one-sided FEMM electric field** just outside the metal, not from the
Kelvin image formula or a direct surface-element integral. Analytical
reference values are never silently substituted into numeric output.

## Reusable viewer

The existing M5 `SamplingContext` and offline HTML/JSON viewer support a
**solid** conductor whose only surface is the outer face. The M7 analytical
and FEMM adapters use identical problem geometry, source-exclusion masks,
conductor metadata, field tracing and density profile export. There is no
scene graph or browser-time FEMM solve. The example exports three stored
charge distances with the same color scale.

```powershell
python examples/femm5_external_sphere.py --output output/external.html
# Requires FEMM5_BINDING and FEMM_DIR:
python examples/femm5_external_sphere.py --backend femm --resolution 25 --output output/external_femm.html
python -m pytest tests/test_m7_live.py -vv -rA
```

## Acceptance gates

The M7 analytical and fake-command suite verified conductor boundary
conditions, image charge and location, integrated induced charge, force from
the energy derivative, signed charges, world-coordinate covariance, strictly
unsupported geometries, cleanup and viewer sampling. Actual pinned FEMM 5 tests compared conductor net charge, source net charge, cavity/metal potential,
exterior potential/field, induced profile and angular charge integral, plus
mesh/domain/source-size sensitivity. Chromium checks confirmed offline
3-state viewer behavior and masks. All previous numerical regressions remain green.

**Historical M7 exclusions (M8 now covers isolated spheres):** multiple external source sites,
conducting planes/rings, arbitrary 3D conductors, near-contact high-resolution
force calculation, live charge motion, production-grade FEMM process isolation,
and uniformly bounded numerical error. The viewer's slider shows static
solutions, not physical trajectories.
