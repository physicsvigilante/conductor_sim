# Current capability and validation map

For a workbook problem, first identify what stays fixed and what the question asks to measure. The routes below distinguish a change between static equilibria from a trajectory with physical time.

The [M41a trial](m41a.md) checked 14 original workbook and notes cases. Twelve had independently checked results for a stated physical model. Two required irregular conductor geometries outside the qualified scope. The trial did not measure educational outcomes or a full-question solve rate.

Admitted `ParallelPlateSolution` local `V`/`E`/`sigma` values are certified within 1 % and sign-correct (uncertified samples raise `OutsideDomain`; empty slabs and on-plate Dirichlet potentials are exact). |

## LLM authoring routing index

Choose the physical contract first, then pick the matching route:
1. **Responding conductors** (metal adjusts its charge distribution to boundary conditions): author `ElectrostaticProblem` or a qualified conductor route below. Never superpose separately solved conductor solutions.
2. **Prescribed nonresponding sources** (fixed charge distributions with no back-reaction): compose providers inside `PrescribedField` where domains overlap.
3. **Topology-only Gauss accounting** (sealed cavities without full local geometry): use `GaussCavityAssembly`.

| Physical arrangement & boundary condition | Exact import & entry point | Supported observables | Key limits / explicit refusals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Centered charge in 1 concentric spherical shell | `conductor_sim`: `ElectrostaticProblem`, `SphericalShell`, `ConcentricShellBackend` | `V`, `E`, face charges | Centered charge only; `Grounded` or `FixedNetCharge` |
| 1 or more 3D charges inside 1 spherical cavity | `conductor_sim`: `ImageChargeShellBackend` (1 charge) / `MultiImageShellBackend` (many); `force_energy` | `V`, `E`, face charges, cavity `force_energy` | All charges inside the single cavity; no exterior charges |
| Strictly nested concentric shells (2–8) + charges in innermost cavity | `conductor_sim`: `NestedShellBackend`; `force_energy` | `V`, `E`, per-face charges, innermost cavity `force_energy` | Charges only in innermost cavity; no annulus/exterior charges |
| Solid conducting sphere + 1 or more exterior 3D charges | `conductor_sim`: `SolidSphere`, `ExternalGroundedSphereBackend`, `ExternalIsolatedSphereBackend`, `ExternalMultiSphereBackend`; `force_energy` | `V`, `E`, `sigma`, `Q_net`, `V_sphere`, `force_energy` | Solid sphere only (no cavities); `Grounded` or `FixedNetCharge` |
| Single grounded conducting plane + 1 or more 3D charges | `conductor_sim`: `ConductingPlane`, `GroundedPlaneBackend`; `force_energy` | `V`, `E`, `sigma`, `force_energy` | `Grounded` only (nonzero `FixedPotential` on one plane is refused) |
| Two facing infinite parallel plates + 0 or more slab charges | `conductor_sim`: `ParallelPlateBackend`; `force_energy` | Certified local `V`/`E`/`sigma` (≤1%, sign-correct), `force_energy` | `Grounded`/`FixedPotential`; uncertified lateral samples raise `OutsideDomain`; infinite-plate total `Q` is `None` |
| Concentric shells or solid-sphere-in-shell wiring / grounding switches | `conductor_sim`: `ConnectedShellBackend`, `MixedRadialBackend`, `ElectricalLink`, `ConnectionPlan`, `prepare_radial_voltages` | Before/after equilibria, `charge_to_ground_c`, charge transfers, radial field-energy release | Radial spherical symmetry (1–8 shells, or 1 solid + shells); static equilibrium pairs only (no transients) |
| Thin mathematical concentric shells (ideal radii) | `conductor_sim.thin_shells`: `ConcentricThinShell`, `solve_thin_shell_charges` | Static net charges under isolated/grounded/floating links | Zero-thickness idealization; no local off-center fields |
| Successive grounding of distant spheres | `conductor_sim.distant_spheres`: `ground_and_disconnect_distant_sphere`, `DistantSphereConnectionBackend` | Sequential potentials and charges | Explicit center-monopole distant-sphere approximation only |
| Exterior field/potential of a spherical outer surface enclosing sealed cavities | `conductor_sim.spherical_exterior`: `SphericalCavityExterior` | Exterior `V`, `E` from cavity ledger | Exterior only; requires spherical outer boundary |
| Sealed multicavity face totals (arbitrary topology) | `conductor_sim.cavity_gauss`: `GaussCavityAssembly`, `solve_cavity_gauss` | Exact entire-face induced totals | Topology-only; refuses local `V`, `E`, `sigma` |
| Disjoint spherical cavities local inner/outer surface density & relocation | `conductor_sim.spherical_multicavity`: `SphericalSigmaModel`, `solve_spherical_sigma`; `conductor_sim.spherical_sigma_relocation`: `relocate_spherical_source`, `compare_static_spherical_relocation` | Pointwise inner/outer `sigma`, static relocation delta | Intentionally refuses general cavity `V`/`E`, forces, and time dynamics |
| Optional axisymmetric numerical FEMM 5 reference | `conductor_sim`: `Femm5Backend`, `Femm5ExternalSphereBackend`, `Femm5PlaneBackend`, `Femm5ParallelPlateBackend` | Axisymmetric `V`, `E`, `sigma`, electrode charges | Requires real FEMM 5 binary; axisymmetric subset only; no FEMM `force_energy` |
| Superposed prescribed nonresponding sources & test-charge motion | `conductor_sim`: `PrescribedField`, `UniformElectricField`, `FixedPointField`, `UniformRingAxisField`, `UniformRingField`, `UniformDiskAxisField`, `UniformRodField`, `UniformSphereField`, `UniformShellField`, `TestChargeMotionSpec`, `integrate_test_charge`, `UniformGravity`, `ConservativeForceSystem` | Superposed `V`, `E`, elapsed-time 1D/2D trajectories, kinetic & `qV` energy | No conductor back-reaction; check ring-axis vs full-ring domain; guards terminate trajectories cleanly |
| Specialized static prescribed-field utilities | `conductor_sim.affine_fields` (`ConservativeAffineField`, `AffineFieldAssembly`), `conductor_sim.parallel_sheets` (`ParallelSheetField`), `conductor_sim.axial_field_graph` (`AxialFieldGraph`), `conductor_sim.radial_field_graph` (`RadialFieldGraph`), `conductor_sim.path_barriers` (`straight_path_energy_barrier`), `conductor_sim` (`CircularArcCharge`, `CoaxialArcAssembly`, `insert_point_charge`, `interaction_energy_j`, `combine_isolated_drops`) | Regional `E`/`V`, signed endpoint work, path energy barriers, pair assembly work, drop coalescence | Standalone specialized routes (not all accept `PrescribedField` composition); verify domain per module |
| Responding-conductor 1D / 2D particle dynamics & sweeps | `conductor_sim`: `Motion1DSpec`, `integrate_motion_1d`, `Motion2DSpec`, `integrate_motion_2d`, `PositionSweepSpec`, `sample_position_sweep` | Elapsed-time trajectories on proved symmetry lines/planes, quasistatic force/work sweeps | One moving charge (+ optional held charges in M18a); no gravity coupling with responding conductors yet |
| Scenario revisions, driven protocols, events & dipoles | `conductor_sim.scenarios` (`PrescribedScenario`, `revise_scenario`), `conductor_sim.structural_scenarios` (`reassemble_scenario`), `conductor_sim.driven_motion` (`DrivenModel`, `investigate_driven_motion`), `conductor_sim.motion_events` (`EventStudy`, `investigate_motion`), `conductor_sim.dipoles` (`RigidDipole`, `DipoleModel`, `observe_dipole`) | Requalified revisions, terminal events, driven switching, dipole torque/work | Prescribed-field regime; every revision requalifies atomically |

**Currently unsupported (refuse explicitly rather than substituting):**
- Hollow conducting shell with an exterior charge, simultaneous interior + exterior charges on one shell, or general local `V`/`E` inside multi-cavity spheres *(planned for M42a)*.
- Point charge in the annulus between concentric shells, or public nonzero `FixedPotential` on bounded spheres/shells *(planned for M42b)*.
- Layered linear dielectrics, conducting sphere/shell in a uniform background field, gravity coupled with responding conductors, arbitrary irregular 3D conductors, and multi-moving-charge dynamics.

## Verification and trust

Report interpretation fidelity, physical correctness, simulation coverage, educational usefulness and optional textbook-answer completeness separately. Count partial/refused cases honestly; never count skipped FEMM tests as numerical FEMM acceptance. Independent review is useful scrutiny, not a guarantee that every defect is absent. A JSON consistency checker does not authenticate separately edited HTML bytes or historical teacher origin without a trusted external anchor.
