# Current capability and validation map

For a workbook problem, first identify what stays fixed and what the question asks to measure. The routes below distinguish a change between static equilibria from a trajectory with physical time.

The [M41a trial](m41a.md) checked 14 original workbook and notes cases. Twelve had independently checked results for a stated physical model. Two required irregular conductor geometries outside the qualified scope. The trial did not measure educational outcomes or a full-question solve rate.

| Layer | Reusable entry points | Qualified scope and limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Responding conductors | `ElectrostaticProblem`, analytical backends, optional FEMM 5, `force_energy` | Grounded/isolated spheres, cavities, shells, planes and other explicitly supported arrangements. Each backend validates the whole geometry and boundary conditions; no arbitrary-conductor solver. |
| Straight-guide energy barrier | `straight_path_energy_barrier` | Finds the maximum potential energy rise along a straight segment in combined affine fields and uniform volume-sphere interiors. [Workbook p3 Q35](straight_path_energy_barrier.md) |
| Point-charge assembly energy | `insert_point_charge`, `interaction_energy_j` | Total pair energy and signed work of bringing one charge from infinity. [Workbook p4 Q37 and Q44](point_charge_assembly.md) |
| Infinite parallel charged sheets | `ParallelSheetField` | Signed regional fields, continuous potentials and endpoint work, with qualified axial gap graphs. [Workbook Q46 and Q48](parallel_sheet_field.md) |
| Axial field graphs | `AxialFieldGraph` | Integrates continuous piecewise-linear fields and superposes them over their common axis interval. [Workbook p4 Q40](axial_field_graph.md) |
| Prescribed spherical volume and surface charge | `PrescribedField`, `endpoint_field_work` | Compare signed work inside and outside a charged body as its charge distribution changes. [Workbook Q50](volume_sphere_work.md) |
| Conservative affine fields | `ConservativeAffineField`, `AffineFieldAssembly` | Static E, potential and signed endpoint work for superposed symmetric-gradient and uniform fields. [Workbook p3 Q28](conservative_affine_fields.md) |
| Ideal concentric conducting shells | `ConcentricThinShell`, `solve_thin_shell_charges` | Static net charges for isolated, grounded and floating-linked mathematical shells at specified radii. [Workbook p3 Q36](concentric_thin_shells.md) |
| Successive grounding of distant spheres | `ground_and_disconnect_distant_sphere` | Named, one-at-a-time grounding in the explicit centre-monopole approximation. [Workbook p3 Q31](successive_distant_grounding.md) |
| Sealed cavities with a spherical outer surface | `SphericalCavityExterior` | Exterior field and potential from the cavity charge ledger and spherical boundary condition. [Workbook p3 Q34 and p4 Q42](spherical_cavity_exterior.md) |
| Conducting drops | [combine_isolated_drops](conducting_drop_equilibria.md) | Combines independently equilibrated spherical drops by conserving charge and volume, then solves the final sphere. Accepts unequal radii and either charge sign. |
| Concentric voltage preparation | `prepare_radial_voltages` | Prepare the named conductors at specified potentials, disconnect their supplies, and pass the isolated state into a grounding or wiring plan. [Workbook p3 Q32](radial_voltage_preparation.md) |
| Grounding switches | `ElectricalLink`, `ConnectionPlan`, `ConnectionResult.charge_to_ground_c` | Signed charge delivered to earth between radial conductor equilibria. A single grounded conductor may share a plan with a separate floating group. [Workbook p14 Q2](single_conductor_ground_switch.md) |
| Prescribed sources | `PrescribedField` and supported point, rod, ring, disk-axis, sphere/shell and uniform-field components | Fixed nonresponding source fields can be superposed where all component domains are valid. Full ring and ring-axis providers have different domains; a prescribed source is not automatically a responding conductor. |
| Coaxial charged arcs | `CircularArcCharge`, `CoaxialArcAssembly` | Total charge on each arc determines potential, axial field component and field work between axial endpoints. The transverse field depends on angular charge density. [Workbook p3 Q33](coaxial_arc_observations.md) |
| Particle observations | `TestChargeMotionSpec`, native trajectory/event/driven APIs, qualified electric/gravity composition | Real elapsed-time integration in certified axes/planes, with guards, terminal events and censored outcomes. Not unrestricted many-body or general 3D motion. |
| Physical/structural revision | `PrescribedScenario`, `revise_scenario`, named-source `reassemble_scenario` | Retained models, stable source IDs, parameter changes and atomic source-inventory revisions. Every revised system is requalified. This identity layer is not a universal schema for every older backend. |
| Sweeps and history | Trajectory/event sweeps, unified observation graphs, forks and fresh event inquiries | Native re-execution, explicit branch lineage and compatible-clock comparisons. M35a multi-stage history and M35b legacy sweep integrity are on this baseline; [issue #80's correction](https://github.com/physicsvigilante/conductor_sim/pull/82) is not still an unimplemented draft. In-process receipts are not external cryptographic authentication. |
| Source-backed JEE replays | M36b/c pilots, M36d/e actual solid-sphere refinement, M37b workbook current example | Source interpretation, teacher numerical choices and optional answers are separately identified. Historical pilot/refusal/partial categories are not a measured 30-question success rate. |
| Prescribed grounding current | M37a/b and M38a/b/c qualified Kelvin protocols/viewers | Current follows the supported prescribed source-position protocol and charge-balance derivative. It is not finite-resistance transient simulation or a freely accelerating source. |
| Multicavity Gauss ledger | M39a `GaussCavityAssembly`, M39b topology viewer | Entire-face induced totals for declared sealed cavities in one connected metal. Topology-only records do not prove physical shape/containment or supply local E/V/sigma. |
| Spherical multicavity density | M40a `SphericalSigmaModel`, M40b `surface_atlas_manifest` / `render_surface_atlas` | Real disjoint spherical cavities, source positions, signed inner point densities and uniform spherical outer density. Original raw rays remain authoritative near walls. No general cavity E/V, source forces or dynamics. |
| Static source relocation | `conductor_sim.spherical_sigma_relocation` provides `relocate_spherical_source` and `compare_static_spherical_relocation` | Compare pointwise induced density after moving one named charge within the same spherical cavity. Small differences can be unresolved. [Relocation model](m40c.md) |
| Presentation | Offline HTML/SVG, actual saved-state playback, source geometry where qualified | Displays existing qualified observations; interpolation is not a solver. M40b is a static XY projection and discrete point atlas with explicit Z coordinates, not a 2D cut or continuous surface solution. |
| LLM authorability | Typed Python models, executable examples, explicit refusal reasons | An LLM can author unfamiliar supported models. Arbitrary text/PDF interpretation and a universal JEE answer engine are not implemented. |

**Historical wording is preserved.** Old milestone documents, frozen charters and PR bodies often say “draft” or “no approval”; those statements describe their original candidate. They do not override a later source-pinned merge/release record. Conversely, a green predecessor does not release a new branch. The package's historical `0.1.0` is not a validated milestone identity.

## Verification and trust

Report interpretation fidelity, physical correctness, simulation coverage, educational usefulness and optional textbook-answer completeness separately. Count partial/refused cases honestly; never count skipped FEMM tests as numerical FEMM acceptance. Independent review is useful scrutiny, not a guarantee that every defect is absent. A JSON consistency checker does not authenticate separately edited HTML bytes or historical teacher origin without a trusted external anchor.
